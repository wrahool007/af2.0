Key findings
All 4 personas achieved measurable empowerment
Average mood gain of +1.9 pts across 12 days. Musician showed fastest gains; News Reader showed the steepest rise in Days 10–12.
Algorithm resistance was real but temporary
All feeds reverted initially. Sustained 3+ day disruption produced visible, lasting feed diversification in every persona.
Section III had the highest empowerment impact
Both mood and control scores peaked during Days 7–9 across all personas — compounding effect of sustained signals.
Emotional impact was most severe for News Reader
Outrage loops and anxiety amplification documented in Day 10. Complete emotional stabilisation achieved by Day 12.
